# Epidemiology emulator handbook

The GLEAM-AI epidemiology emulator forecasts influenza-like illness under intervention scenarios. Inputs describe the target location or node set, intervention timing and duration, intervention intensity, and forecast horizon. Outputs provide baseline and intervention incidence trajectories, peak timing and magnitude, cumulative incidence, and differences between scenarios.

The emulator can directly verify quantitative claims about simulated disease trajectories and intervention effects. It cannot directly verify clinical mechanisms, ethical or policy preferences, unrelated diseases, or causal claims not represented by an input intervention and simulator output.
