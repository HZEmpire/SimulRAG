# SUMO urban-mobility handbook

The SUMO adapter runs microscopic traffic scenarios. Inputs specify a network/scenario, traffic demand or vehicle flow, routing and signal settings, simulation duration, and optional intervention parameters. Outputs include vehicle travel time, waiting time, speed, throughput, queue length, emissions, and scenario-level aggregates.

The simulator can directly verify quantitative traffic outcomes and comparisons between configured scenarios. It cannot directly verify social, legal, equity, or policy claims unless they are explicitly operationalized by the available traffic inputs and outputs.
